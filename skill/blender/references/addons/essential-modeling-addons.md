# 6 Powerful FREE Blender Addons For 3D Modeling

These essential add-ons are completely free and supercharge your 3D modeling workflow:

1. **Edge Flow**: Fixes collapsed curvature and straightens edge flows. Use `Right Click -> Set Flow` to perfectly restore the curve of a deleted edge loop or restore a bevel's interpolation on a curved surface.
2. **Mesh Align Plus**: Replaces tedious manual rotations. Use `Align Planes Easy Mode` to select 3 source vertices and 3 target vertices to snap objects together perfectly in 3D space, preserving scale.
3. **Tissue**: Instancing magic. Generate a repeating pattern within a perfect square, select it, shift-select a target grid, and hit `Tessellate (Quad or Patch)` to wrap your complex pattern flawlessly over the target geometry.
4. **Loop Tools (Built-in)**: Enable this in preferences. `Right Click -> Loop Tools`. 
   - **Circle**: Turns any selection of faces into a perfect circle.
   - **Curve**: Smooths out vertex transitions or creates perfectly straight lines between two points.
   - **Relax / Space**: Evenly spaces out vertices without breaking the overall volume.
5. **Quick Snap**: Press `Ctrl + Shift + V` to bypass Blender's clunky snap menu. Quickly snap vertices to vertices, edges to edges, or even World Origins (`O`) from one object to another across the scene.
6. **Re-Primitive**: Fixes a huge Blender frustration. Hit `F9` to change primitive divisions (like the number of sides on a cylinder) *even after* you have moved or rotated the object in your scene!
