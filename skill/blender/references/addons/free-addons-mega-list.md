# 30 Free Blender Addons for Modeling

A mega-list of free add-ons to boost your productivity:

**CAD & Precision**
- **CAD Sketcher**: 2D sketching tools with constraints (tangents, angles) powered by Geo Nodes.
- **CAD Transform**: Precision transformations and accurate snapping options.
- **Auto Constraints**: Automatically adds axis constraints to move, tweak, and rotate tools.

**Procedural & Generation**
- **BagaPie**: Essential tool for scattering assets, architectural presets, and random arrays.
- **By-Gen**: Non-destructive generative modeling via modifier stacks.
- **Custom Planet**: Generate customizable planets via Geometry Nodes.
- **Block Blender**: Turn 3D models into Minecraft blocks.
- **Building Generator**: Quick preset buildings for background elements.
- **Real Terrain**: 35 different styles of customizable terrains.
- **Sci-Fi Panel Generator**: Fully procedural futuristic panels.
- **Shape Muse**: Intricate Voronoi and honeycomb patterns for 3D printing.

**Assets & Environment**
- **Kops (Free)**: Insert pre-made booleans (doors, windows, hard-surface details).
- **Archipack**: Classic architectural elements (walls, windows, stairs) with real-time adjustments.
- **Vegetation**: Scatter plants dynamically on your scenes.
- **Q Blocker**: Draw primitives directly on the surface of other objects (great for blockouts).

**Cables & Cloth**
- **Geo Cables**: Generate complex hanging procedural pipes and cables.
- **Geometry Nodes Pipes**: Generate pipes along custom curves with valves and connectors.
- **Cloth Simulation (by Daniel Bad)**: UI wrapper for setting up cloth physics, collisions, and pressure.

**Characters & Retopology**
- **Retopo MT**: Draw lines across high-poly models to quickly build retopologized mesh loops.
- **GBH Tool**: Simplifies the new hair system for procedural curves, hair cards, and stylized meshes.

**Utility & Export**
- **Auto Smooth**: Restores the old Auto Smooth modifier behavior in Blender 4.1+.
- **Engles Addon**: Automates exports, naming conventions, and low/high poly suffixes for game engines.
- **LOD Generator**: Generates multiple Levels of Detail (LODs) for game assets to save performance.
- **ND (Non-Destructive)**: UI enhancement for modifier workflows and booleans.
- **Blueprint**: Renders models as stylized white-line blueprints.
